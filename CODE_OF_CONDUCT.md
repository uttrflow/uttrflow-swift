# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a small project and there is no committee — the maintainer decides, and
would rather spend the time on the app.

## The longer version

We want participating in Uttrflow to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their
experience, gender, identity, disability, appearance, background or beliefs.

**Expected:** be welcoming to newcomers, accept that other people have different
constraints from yours, argue about the code rather than the person, and assume the other
person is trying to help until you have real evidence otherwise.

**Not accepted:** harassment of any kind, sexualised language or imagery, personal or
political attacks, publishing someone's private information, or sustained disruption.

## Scope

Anywhere this project happens — issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone
is representing the project.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer directly — [@naveen-bhatt](https://github.com/naveen-bhatt) — rather
than raising it in public. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept private.

What happens next is proportionate and at the maintainer's discretion: usually a private
word, sometimes an edited or removed comment, occasionally a block. There is no appeals
process, because there is no committee to appeal to.
