One thing, parked at the edge of the screen, growing rightwards from its anchor. It never takes focus, so the app you are dictating into stays active throughout. Press and hold the button, or hold the shortcut — both do the same thing and both start with the same soft tick so you know it heard you before you start talking.

Listening, working and inserting are the same 32 points tall, so nothing moves at the moment you let go of the key, and nothing moves again until the words are in. The tick is a claim about the words, so it waits for them. Three of the outcomes are the same 26-point disc, because a success needs no words — when the text has landed you are already looking at it. Only the state with something to do about it is wide, and after a run of discs that width is the message.
