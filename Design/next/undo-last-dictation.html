Undo the last dictation

Proposal for #4206, not built. Today the only way to take back a dictation that landed in the wrong field is to find that app and use its own undo. Three places get one undo action: the menu bar, a shortcut, the dock disc. It removes the text Uttrflow just typed and nothing else; if the field has changed since, it says so and does nothing. Each figure is labelled CURRENT (the shipped page) or PROPOSED.

1 · Menu bar

2 · Dock disc after an insertion

3 · Shortcut

A fifth action beside dictate, clipboard, paste last and copy last: ⌃⌥Z is the proposed default (the owner picks the keys; it is rebindable in Settings → Shortcuts like the others). It does nothing when there is no dictation less than a few minutes old, and shows the same “Couldn’t undo” note as the menu if the field changed.
