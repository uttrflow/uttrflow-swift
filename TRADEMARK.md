# Name, logo and marks

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the **code**. It does not grant any right to the
Uttrflow name or the Uttrflow logo, and MIT has no trademark clause of its own — which is
why this file exists.

**You may** fork this repository, build it, modify it and ship what you build, including
commercially. That is the whole point of the licence.

**Please do not** ship your fork under the name "Uttrflow", or with the Uttrflow mark
(`Design/uttrflow.icns`, `Design/uttrflow.iconset/`, `Design/uttrflow-logo*.png`,
`Sources/Uttrflow/Resources/uttrflow-logo.png`, `Sources/Uttrflow/Resources/MenuBarIconTemplate*.png`)
as its icon or its brand. Give it your own name and your own mark, so that nobody
downloading it believes they are getting the thing served by `api.uttrflow.com`.

That is the only restriction, and it is about identity rather than about code: a user who
cannot tell a fork from the original cannot make an informed decision about what is
reading their microphone.

## Third-party marks

Provider sign-in marks are **not** in this repository and are not covered by anything
here — they belong to their owners, on their owners' terms. `Scripts/fetch-provider-marks.sh`
fetches them from the source each provider publishes for exactly this purpose. The app
builds and runs without them; the buttons carry their wording alone.
